## Q1. Library-core convention: where the loop lives (RULING)

The loop is upstream: `from langchain.agents import AgentState, create_agent` (libs/deepagents/deepagents/graph.py:11) and `return create_agent(model, ..., middleware=deepagent_middleware, ...)` (graph.py:961). Todo-planning was even removed from the in-tree default stack (CHANGELOG.md:163 - `TodoListMiddleware` is now opt-in, only re-added in the Codex harness profile profiles/harness/_openai_codex.py:77).

**Ruling (becomes the convention for library-core subjects):** a subject whose agent loop lives in a published upstream library is scored on what the in-tree product plane actually enforces - assembly order, middleware stack composition, state-model choices, backend abstraction, permission wiring - with NO docking for the absent raw loop and NO credit for the loop's own properties. Symmetric with the openhands row (out-of-tree core earns zero credit AND takes zero blame). Two ceilings attach:
1. Architecture's top rungs ("loop/state model separated from every product surface") can be credited at most to 8 for library-core subjects, because the separation is only verifiable up to the `create_agent` boundary - the review cannot audit loop internals for state-model claims made upstream.
2. Any loop-level verification claim (e.g. "approval binds in-loop") counts only for in-tree code, not for langchain's own middleware.

Why credit and not penalize: the middleware stack IS the product here - create_deep_agent assembles required-middleware protection (graph.py:259-269), exclusion coverage verification (graph.py:615-623), profile-driven harness behavior, and a backend protocol with 8+ implementations (backends/: state, filesystem, sandbox, composite, store, context_hub, langsmith, local_shell + daytona/modal/runloop/quickjs partners). The TUI (libs/code), ACP connector (libs/acp), and talon runtime all consume exactly this assembly. Treating that as "no architecture" would score the pi-vendoring kimi family on pi's loop too - incoherent. Treating it as full loop credit would let a thin wrapper claim codex rungs. Credit the assembly, cap at 8, dock normally for god files: architecture **7** (see Q3).
