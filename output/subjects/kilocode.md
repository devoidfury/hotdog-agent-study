### token-economy — 8 (weight 10, lane-econ)

## Score-disagreement resolution

- **Anchor split (cline vs nanocoder vs pi).** The three lanes anchored against different subjects because each anchored its own slice: core→cline (architecture/operability profile), safety→nanocoder (only other jail-off-by-default subject), econ→pi (cache-as-mechanism instinct). Adopted closest_anchor: **cline** — it is the anchor the two weight-15 dimensions both dock against (dual-tree, verification-8 ceiling), and kilocode's score vector is nearest cline's (deltas: arch −1, safety +1, token +1, originality +1, durability −1). Nanocoder stays useful only as the safety 5-rung contrast (fail-open exec, no SECURITY.md); pi as the token-economy rung.
- **Transcript import counted twice?** Core credited it to operability, econ to interop. Same fact, different dimensions, one merged finding (kilocode-m1); neither lane double-counted within its own dimension. Accepted.
- **Fork-overlay: dock (core) vs credit (safety).** Core docked architecture for the in-place patched loop; safety credited the merge-hazard controls (workflow allowlist, annotation CI). Not a contradiction: those controls bound the hazard's blast radius (safety/verification credit) without removing the hazard from the loop's control flow (architecture dock stands). No score changed.
- **Dual-tree docked in two dimensions.** Core docked architecture (c2) and econ docked docs-dx (e10, surface ambiguity). Same fact, different dimensions; recorded so the fact is visibly double-used, and deliberately not double-docked within either dimension.
- **Verification 9 refusals agree.** Safety withheld 9 for release-gated evals + no fuzzing; core's operability-9 withhold (no documented journal) is a different axis. No dispute to arbitrate; both align with the frozen anchor ceiling.
- **Safety 7 vs the cline 6 and nanocoder 5 rungs.** kilocode gets a full rung above cline/pi/crush (real jail, fail-closed, human-only escalation, monotonic config — mechanisms enumerated at file:line and absent at that rung) but nanocoder's placement below 6 is not inherited: kilocode's off-by-default jail fails closed, is documented honestly, and is violation-tested in CI, inverting every defect that put nanocoder at 5.

## Upstream-comparison ruling (rule (a))

kilocode (77.5) **outranks its upstream opencode (77.0)**. This is permitted only for a divergent fork, and the scout's provenance verdict — divergent-fork, not sync-fork: own product surface, ~67.7k LOC Kilo-original, renamed manifest, 740 marker files with active sync tooling — supports the distinction. The differential is recorded per dimension, never silent: **+1 safety-enforcement** (7 vs 6: a fail-closed OS jail, human-only escalation and an SNI-pinned egress proxy that upstream lacks entirely), **+1 orchestration** (8 vs 7: durable bounded board, daemon supervision, worktree-family management), **−1 architecture** (7 vs 8: the fork-overlay loop, dual live engines, 3x message-part.tsx and the 6,110-LOC webview host — all kilocode-specific debt the upstream design does not carry). Net +0.5. If a future census reclassifies kilocode as a sync-fork, rule (a) caps it at opencode's band ceiling.

## Verdict

**Strongest: token-economy (8)** — tied numerically with verification, orchestration, interop, operability, originality and docs-dx, but it is the one dimension with integrator-verified evidence exceeding its anchor's own mechanism at the same rung: the trigger prices the exact serialized wire payload and blocks the request pre-send (overflow.ts:101-122; llm.ts:159-170) — strictly more faithful than pi's projected estimator — and cache stability is engineered into prompt-content design (byte-identical pinned blocks, prompt.ts:391-406), which cline explicitly lacks. **Weakest: architecture (7)** — the product's control-flow heart is a 2,673-LOC upstream file rewritten in 69 marked places, exit predicates included (prompt.ts:1560, :1604-1617), with two session engines mutating one store (sdk.gen.ts:5047 vs :10030); a wrong conflict resolution during the frequent upstream sync silently changes when the agent stops. (safety-enforcement ties at 7, but the architecture dock carries weight 15.)
