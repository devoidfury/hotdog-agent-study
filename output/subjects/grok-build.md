## Score-disagreement resolution (explicit)

The three lanes score disjoint dimensions, so there are no direct numeric conflicts; four real judgment tensions existed and are resolved here:
1. **Architecture 8.5 vs the god-file errata.** Lane-core scored an 8→9-grade separation criterion but docked 0.5 for file-scale residue; lane-safety meanwhile observed that the worst-looking god file, `permission/manager/mod.rs` (7,781), is mostly test discipline (tests run from :1510). Resolution: hold 8.5. The pager errata dock stands independently (extensions_modal.rs 8,021 et al.), and the permission manager's size is not debt evidence either way — it was never the dock's basis.
2. **The leader daemon is credited and distrusted by different lanes.** Core counts it toward operability 9 (resident sessions, crash-restart supervisor); safety notes the sandbox *disables* leader mode rather than confining it (`pager-bin/main.rs:1358-1372`) and the hub/leader trust boundary is unaudited. Resolution: the feature earns operability credit once; the risk is expressed as withheld safety credit within the existing 7, not as a second dock on operability. No score moves; flagged for a future multi-process-surface pass.
3. **Verification convergence at the 8-ceiling.** All three lanes independently declined to go past 8 (core: "no in-CI evals/fuzzing visible"; econ: "no in-CI eval evidence exists to lift anything"; safety: anchors' errata rule, CI unknown-due-to-export). Unanimous 8, one declared method: scored on in-repo harness evidence, with the one affirmative CI signal in-code (`sandbox/Cargo.toml:28` self-skip comment).
4. **Token-economy 9 = codex-parity claim.** Lane-econ asserts parity plus prefire; the counter-argument (no in-repo effectiveness numbers) is exactly why prefire earns uniqueness but no extra half-rung. Hold 9.
Scout-vs-lane: none — scout predicted no scores, only risks, and every flagged top-risk (test-LOC undercount, no CI, default-off sandbox, manager god-file) was dispositioned by a lane.
